Patika starts with what you tell it. Not a mood picker or a quiz score, but your own words about what is weighing on you. From that, it builds a guided meditation plan for you: a path of 7, 14, 21 or 28 days, one short audio step a day, with an end you can see.

HOW IT WORKS

- Say it your way. Choose what is closest (sleep, anxiety, burnout, focus, pressure before exams or work, social situations, being hard on yourself, a breakup or loss, anger, or something you cannot quite name), then describe it in a sentence or two.
- Get your path. Patika plans a sequence of sessions around what you shared, when it tends to show up, and how long you have been carrying it. You see the whole route before you begin.
- Listen, one step a day. Each session is voice-guided audio. The opening and closing speak to your situation; the practice itself, like breath counting or a body scan, stays steady and simple.
- See what changed. Short check-ins at the start, along the way and at the end let you compare where you are with where you began. No clinical tests, no scores to chase.

A PATH THAT ENDS

Most meditation apps are open-ended libraries. Patika gives you a single path with a clear finish. When it ends, you get an honest look at how it went, and you decide what comes next.

MORE ROOM TO PRACTICE

- Discover: ten prepared seven-step paths, from Room to breathe and Evening, unhurried to Kinder to yourself and Carrying it with you. The audio is included and works offline.
- Daily reminder at the hour you choose. Notifications never mention your path or what you shared.
- Audio keeps playing with the screen locked.
- Notes and quiet milestones in your profile. A gentle weekly rhythm with no streak to lose and no guilt when you miss a day.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

- Start without an account. Link Sign in with Apple or Google later if you want your path on another device.
- What you write is stored encrypted. It is never sent to analytics or used for advertising.
- Lock the app with Face ID.
- Export your data or delete your account from Settings at any time.

SUPPORT IS ALWAYS THERE

Get support is visible throughout the app, is never behind a purchase, and shows crisis line numbers for your country, with a directory of local helplines. If what you write suggests you may be in danger, Patika pauses and shows you those numbers first.

Patika is a wellbeing tool for adults. It is not therapy, medical care or a diagnosis, and it is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
