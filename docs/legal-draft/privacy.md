# Patika Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [publication date]

Patika is operated by **[legal operator name]**. Contact us at
**[public support email]**. Patika offers structured mental well-being
programs for adults. It is not a clinical assessment, therapy or emergency
service.

## Information we handle

We process the problem statement you choose to enter, your program choices,
session progress, brief check-ins and notes to create and deliver your path.
Your original problem statement is stored separately in encrypted form.
We use a shorter summary when requesting generated content where possible.
Account identifiers and authentication details let us keep your path and
purchase access associated with your account. Payment card details are
handled by Apple, not Patika.

We use product event data to understand reliability and use, without sending
your raw problem statement or measurement answers to analytics. We do not
sell your sensitive information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage and server functions.
Google Gemini supports path generation. ElevenLabs provides generated speech.
RevenueCat processes purchase events and product access records. Apple
processes App Store purchases. PostHog receives privacy-limited product events
when enabled. Apple or Google may handle sign-in if you choose those options.

## Purchases and account linking

You may buy a path while using an anonymous account. We associate access with
that account's Patika identifier. A consumable App Store purchase does not
reappear automatically from the store receipt on a new device. Link an account
before changing devices to make access to your server-held path reliable.
Contact support if you need help with a purchase. We do not use your problem
text, path title or measurement answers as RevenueCat paywall variables.

## Your choices and rights

You can export available profile data and request account deletion in the app.
Deleting an account removes the associated server data subject to legal
retention obligations. Contact **[public support email]** for access,
correction or deletion questions. Turn off notifications in the app or device
settings. Notification text does not include your problem or path name.

## Retention and security

We retain data while your account is active and for **[verified retention
period]** after deletion where required for fraud, tax or legal obligations.
We restrict access, use encryption for sensitive input and deliver private
audio through time-limited links. No system can guarantee absolute security.
