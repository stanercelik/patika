# Patika Support

For help with access, purchases, account linking, data export or account
deletion, contact **[public support email]**. Include your purchase date and
App Store transaction ID if available. Do not send your problem statement,
measurement answers or other sensitive details by email.

**Restore purchases:** On the same Patika account, use Restore purchases in
the paywall. Consumable purchases do not reliably restore from the App Store
receipt on a different device. Linking an account preserves access to the
path grant stored by Patika. If access has not appeared after payment, wait
for verification and then contact support.

**Refunds:** Request App Store refunds through Apple. Patika will update
paid access when the refund is confirmed. Your notes and measurements remain
available according to the privacy policy.

Patika is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, contact local
emergency services. The app's Destek al route is free.
